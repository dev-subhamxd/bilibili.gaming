THE ORIGIN

Bilibili Gaming is a Chinese esports organization owned by Bilibili.com — the platform that turned scrolling bullet comments into a national pastime. The Valorant division launched in February 2023, and by the end of that same month the roster was already fighting through open qualifiers.

Six months later, BLG closed their rookie Champions Tour campaign at 7th–8th place at Valorant Champions 2023 in Los Angeles, banking $50,000 against rosters with years of a head start. It set the tone: BLG doesn't rebuild quietly. They reload in public, on stream, in China's most contested region.

Since then the org has swung between heartbreak and hardware — a runners-up finish at VCT 2024 China Kickoff, a Stage 2 title in 2025, an Evolution Series trophy in 2026 — every arc playing out in front of the platform that made them.
